Q: "API returns 401 Unauthorized"
A: Include auth header: -H "Authorization: Bearer demo_key_12345"

Q: "Low detection rate (< 80%)"
A: Lower detection_threshold in config/settings.yaml

Q: Module import errors?
A: Ensure you're in project root and venv is activated
